About the Creator

Mahjong has been part of my life for as long as I can remember.

What started as childhood fascination eventually became Mahjong IQ — a strategy tool designed to help players better understand the possibilities hidden in every rack.

The Beginning

I just wanted
to be part of it.

When I was growing up, my mother regularly hosted mahjong games with her friends, and I was completely fascinated by it all — the sound of the tiles, the laughter, the energy around the table, and the feeling that something important and exciting was happening.

More than anything, I wanted to be part of it.

The problem was, I became so obsessed with staying up to watch the games that I refused to go to bed. Eventually, my mother had to stop hosting because I simply would not leave the games alone.

Years later, as an adult looking for more meaningful and intimate ways to gather with friends, it became my turn to truly learn the game.

I still play with my grandmother's vintage mahjong set, which was passed down to my mother and eventually to me. One day, I hope to pass it down to my daughter.

Over the years, these tiles have been part of countless games, endless laughter, spirited competition, and long nights around the table.

They've also been used to test nearly every iteration of Mahjong IQ along the way, helping me make sure the strategy and logic behind the app truly reflected the way real games unfold.

Why I Created Mahjong IQ

The hardest part of mahjong isn't learning the rules.
It's knowing where to go.

At the start of every game, there are dozens of possible hands and countless ways to interpret your tiles.

Some players instinctively recognize direction quickly. Others feel overwhelmed by the possibilities or commit too early to a hand that limits their chances later.

The Hand Analyzer was created to help players slow that moment down — to see stronger possibilities, recognize smarter paths, and build confidence before the first discard.

The Thinking Behind It

Strategy begins with seeing patterns clearly.

Over time, my fascination with the game evolved into a fascination with decision-making itself — how experienced players quickly recognize opportunities, pivot when needed, and instinctively understand probability and flow.

The Hand Analyzer was designed around that same idea: helping players better recognize what their tiles are actually telling them.
