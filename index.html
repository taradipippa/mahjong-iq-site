Your top hands, ranked and explained.

Enter your tiles and instantly see your top 3 hand options. Each hand is ranked by how well it fits — based on how many of your tiles already support it, and how difficult it will be to get the remaining tiles to complete the hand.

Tap See Why on any hand to go deeper:

  • What you have & what you need — tile by tile, with progress on each group

Playing on your own

Practice. Compare. Improve.

Playing with a group

Learn Together. Play Together.

This app is great for group play when everyone has access. Enter your tiles at the start of the game and begin with a clear strategy from the first draw. Perfect for tables of newer players learning and improving together.
