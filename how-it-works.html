How It Works

Mahjong is a game of patterns, strategy, and fast decisions.

Knowing which hand to play is where most players get stuck.

The American Mahjong hand analyzer that shows you your strongest options before the first tile is thrown.

With 70+ possible hands on the card, identifying the strongest direction quickly can feel overwhelming — especially early in the game.

The Mahjong IQ hand analyzer analyzes your tiles and helps you identify the hands with the strongest potential — so you can make smarter decisions from the very beginning of the game.

01

Enter Your Tiles

Enter your starting tiles using the interactive tile selector. The analyzer evaluates your hand and instantly identifies the most promising directions based on:

  • Tile fit
  • Hand flexibility
  • Joker compatibility
  • Calling potential
  • Overall difficulty

Simple to use. Powerful from the very first game.

02

See Your Strongest Paths

The analyzer ranks your strongest hand opportunities based on your current tiles, helping you focus on the directions with the highest potential.

Rather than scanning the entire card, you'll start with a handful of realistic options worth pursuing.

Your Top Priority Hands

The hands with the highest overall potential based on your current tiles.

Next Three Hands to Consider

Alternative directions worth keeping open as the game develops.

Pivot Opportunities

Hands that become attractive if key tiles begin arriving.

The goal isn't to lock you into one hand.

It's to help you think strategically from the beginning.

03

See Why Each Hand Works

When you expand a hand, Mahjong IQ breaks down the strategy behind it.

Understand exactly what you have, what you're missing, and how close you are to completing the hand.

What You Have & What You Need

See your progress tile by tile and understand exactly what's still required.

Ready to Call

Identify which groups are already complete and can be called immediately.

Joker Placement

See where your jokers can strengthen the hand and improve flexibility.

The more you understand the reasoning, the stronger your instincts become.

04

Make Smarter Discards

Every discard shapes the direction of your game.

Mahjong IQ helps identify which tiles contribute least to your strongest hand paths so you can discard with greater confidence and less second-guessing.

Over time, you'll begin recognizing these patterns naturally — even without the analyzer.

That's when the game starts to click.

Mahjong IQ isn't just a tool.

The more you use it, the more you'll begin to recognize strong opportunities on your own.

You'll start to understand:

  • Which hands are realistically achievable
  • When flexibility matters more than commitment
  • Why some hands are harder than they appear
  • How experienced players identify strong directions quickly

You're not memorizing combinations.

You're developing pattern recognition.
