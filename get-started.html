When should I use the Hand Analyzer?

If you're playing by yourself, a great way to practice is to pick some tiles, decide which hands you'd play in a real game, then hit Analyze and see how your thinking compares. You'll learn a lot from the guidance.

If you're playing with others, make sure everyone at the table has access so no one has an unfair advantage. The best time to use it together is before, during, and/or after the charleston — before the first tile is cast.

I got a new phone or cleared my browser. Now what?

Come back to getmahjongiq.com/get-started.html first — this page remembers your purchase most of the time and will get you right back in. If you're on a brand new device and it's not recognizing you, just email tara@getmahjongiq.com with your Stripe receipt and we'll restore your access.

Does it work without wifi?

Yes. Once you've opened the Hand Analyzer on your device, it works even without a connection — perfect for playing at someone's house or anywhere with spotty signal.

Is this a one-time purchase or a subscription?

One-time. You pay $14.99 once and get unlimited access to the 2026 Hand Analyzer — no recurring charges, no expiration. It's yours to keep for as long as you want.

What happens when next year's hands come out?

Your purchase gives you unlimited access to the 2026 Hand Analyzer for as long as you want — it never expires. When the 2027 hands are released, a 2027 Hand Analyzer will be available for purchase separately. You're never required to upgrade.

What's your refund policy?

Because you can try the Hand Analyzer free before you buy, we don't offer refunds as a general policy. That said, if you ran into a technical problem we couldn't solve, reach out at tara@getmahjongiq.com and we'll make it right.
